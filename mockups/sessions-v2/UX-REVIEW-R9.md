Four reviewers checked the round 8 mockup (`option-ab-merged/index.html` and `mobile.html`) with agent-browser: icons, motion, desktop feel, and phone feel.
One fixer applied every P0 and P1, plus the cheap P2s, to the mockup (round 9). The column "Where" points at round 8 line numbers.
Use this file as the implementation checklist for apps/ui. The "Fix" column names the shipped primitive or token to use.

Skipped by the fixer (apps/ui work, or a decision for Taras):

## icons

The icon set is clean on the surface, but the status glyphs (spinner, red "!" disc, outline check, dot chip) are hand-rolled. They do not match the shipped TaskStatusIcon/StatusBadge family, and several glyphs mean two things: CircleCheck is both Done and completed, Clock is Later, deferred, waiting and Schedules, and a dashed ring is both No group and backlog. Swapping in the shipped lifecycle icons removes most of these collisions. The other fixes are AgentAvatar for workers, a few lucide renames (Archive, AlarmClock, SquareDashed, Hexagon, Columns2) and two missing assets: a Telegram logo and a Checkbox primitive.

## motion

The motion layer is close in spirit: hover linger on rows and tabs, 150 ms chevrons, and a reduced-motion block exist. But it is off the DESIGN.md contract in the places users feel most. There is one real bug (the desktop toast jumps 113 px when its entrance ends), no overlay has an exit, sheets and the phone push use a non-token curve with an ease-in close, and the worker-line expand and the typing line snap and shift layout. Reduced motion zeroes everything instead of keeping fades. The build should take its timings from the shipped primitives (AnimatedReveal fast, Dialog 200/150, Popover 150/100, Sheet 400/250 ease-swift, Sonner) plus one missing primitive (a vaul Drawer), and should not port the mockup CSS.

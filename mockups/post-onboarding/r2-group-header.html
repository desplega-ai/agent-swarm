Group header: the compact strip

A use case is a Sessions group. Its header is one 48px strip under the group title: the schedules, a Run now button, the outputs and a way to ask the Lead. A click on a segment opens a drawer under the strip, in the page, not in a modal. The session list under it keeps today's chrome, so the page still reads as a session list first.

  • Scales to 50+ schedules. The strip shows a count and the next run. The drawer has search, a status filter and one row per schedule with its own Run now.
  • Outputs are the files under the group key, newest first, with an inline preview. Ask the Lead opens a composer scoped to the group.
  • Unchanged: the filter bar (All, Unread, Running, Needs you), the grid columns and the pager.

The desktop states need a window wider than 760px. The phone frames follow.

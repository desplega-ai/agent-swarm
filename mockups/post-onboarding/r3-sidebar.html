Sidebar: inset panels, two ways to group

The sidebar and the page are now two panels on the window canvas. Each panel has 12px corners and a hairline border, 8px from the window edge and from each other. The groups change in two places: Templates leaves Build, and Schedules and Workflows move out of Build.

  • Inset. In code: shadcn variant="floating" on the Sidebar, plus m-2 ml-0 rounded-xl border on SidebarInset. The canvas is one new token.
  • Two variants. Variant 1 puts Schedules and Workflows in Work. Variant 2 gives them a group named Automate. Build holds Agents, Tasks, Skills, Memory and Scripts, and is collapsed on a first visit.
  • No width jump. Every scroll container keeps an 8px gutter (scrollbar-gutter: stable). The sidebar uses that gutter as its right padding, so a row is the same width with or without a scrollbar.
  • Settings flyout. Eight entries, then Advanced: Secrets, Templates, Connection playground and Raw fetch credentials.

The desktop states need a window wider than 760px. The phone sheet follows.

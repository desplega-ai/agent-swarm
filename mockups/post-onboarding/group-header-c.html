All variants
IdeaThe session list keeps its full height. Schedule and Outputs live in a 300px right rail (the detail-page rail pattern). The chat box docks at the bottom of the list, like a conversation you add to.
Trade-offBest use of a wide screen and the list never moves. But it narrows the list on laptops, collides with the Sessions split view (a third column), and on phone the rail has to turn into chips plus a sheet.

Desktop states are hidden on narrow screens. Widen the window to see them.

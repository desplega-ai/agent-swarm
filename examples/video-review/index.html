Draw Timeline 1x
Timeline. Drag in the empty area to make a range. Drag a range to move it. Drag its ends to trim it. Right click for options. Click the ruler to move the playhead. Cmd+scroll or pinch to zoom. Drag the bar above the timeline to change its height.
Picture. Drag to draw. Click to play or pause.
Keys. Space play, ← → frame (with Shift: second), ↑ ↓ previous or next comment, I O range in and out, P A B pen, arrow, box, C write, Cmd+Enter finish the comment, Delete remove, Esc deselect, + - 0 zoom, Cmd+Z undo.
